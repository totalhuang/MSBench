Transition definitions:
* Hard cut: one shot changes to the next instantaneously, without a visible transition interval.
* Dissolve (cross-dissolve): the outgoing and incoming shot images visibly overlap while one loses opacity and the other gains opacity.
* Fade: a shot fades toward a substantially uniform color, usually black or white, or emerges from that color. A fade-to-black/white followed by a fade-in must visibly pass through the requested color.
* Wipe: a moving boundary or shape progressively replaces one shot with the next. Camera movement or an object moving within the same continuous shot is not sufficient.
* Other transition: a visible transition from one shot or scene to a different one that is none of the four types above, for example the camera pushes or flies through an object or opening into a new scene, one scene morphs into another, or a shape-matched or graphic effect links the two scenes.
* No transition: the interval stays within one continuous shot. Only camera motion, object motion, lighting, focus or exposure changes happen, and no different shot or scene replaces the current one.
