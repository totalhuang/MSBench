Transition definitions:
* Hard cut: one shot changes to the next instantaneously, without a visible transition interval. Match cuts, which link the two shots by similar visual content, are hard cuts; camera motion before or after the change does not make it gradual.
* Dissolve (cross-dissolve): the outgoing and incoming shot images visibly overlap while one loses opacity and the other gains opacity.
* Fade: a shot fades toward a mostly uniform color, usually black or white, or emerges from that color. A fade-out or a fade-in alone is sufficient.
* Wipe: a moving boundary or shape, of any direction or form, progressively replaces one shot with the next. Camera movement or an object moving within the same continuous shot is not sufficient.
